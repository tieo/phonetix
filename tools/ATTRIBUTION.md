# Data in these packs

The dictionary packs (`*.pack`) are built from the English Wiktionary, as extracted by
wiktextract and published at https://kaikki.org/.

Wiktionary content is available under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0
licence (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/) and the GNU Free Documentation
License. The packs are a derived work and are distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0.

Wiktionary: https://en.wiktionary.org/ · wiktextract: https://github.com/tatuylonen/wiktextract

`models.json` lists translation models published by Mozilla for Firefox Translations; the
files are downloaded from Mozilla, not redistributed here.
